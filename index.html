Important milestones

Nigeria's Historical Timeline

Follow key events that influenced the development of Nigeria.

c. 1500 BCE onward

Nok Culture

The Nok culture is famous for its terracotta sculptures and early ironworking. Its dates and development remain subjects of research.

c. 1100–1400 CE

Ile-Ife's Artistic Traditions

Ile-Ife became a major Yoruba cultural and political centre, renowned for extraordinary naturalistic sculptures.

Before colonial rule

Powerful African States

The Benin Kingdom, Oyo Empire, Kanem-Bornu and other states developed distinctive political systems, trade networks and cultures.

1804 onward

Fulani Jihad and Sokoto Caliphate

The movement led by Usman dan Fodio transformed political and religious life across much of northern Nigeria.

1914

Amalgamation of Nigeria

British colonial authorities joined the Northern and Southern Protectorates into one administrative colony under Frederick Lugard.

1 October 1960

Independence

Nigeria gained independence from Britain, beginning a new chapter as a sovereign country.

1 October 1963

Nigeria Becomes a Republic

Nigeria became a republic, replacing the British monarch as its ceremonial head of state.

1967–1970

Nigerian Civil War

The conflict, also known as the Biafran War, caused immense suffering and remains an important part of national history.

29 May 1999

Return to Civilian Rule

Nigeria returned to civilian democratic government after years of military rule.

Today

Modern Nigeria

Nigeria continues to develop through its diverse communities, democratic institutions, arts, education, technology and economic activity.

People who shaped our story

Historical Figures

Meet some of the people associated with important moments in Nigerian history.

Nationalist

Nnamdi Azikiwe

A leading nationalist and Nigeria's first president after the country became a republic in 1963.

Nationalist

Herbert Macaulay

An important early Nigerian nationalist who campaigned against aspects of British colonial rule.

Nationalist

Obafemi Awolowo

A major political leader who advocated federalism and expanded access to education in the Western Region.

Nationalist

Ahmadu Bello

The Sardauna of Sokoto and premier of Northern Nigeria, influential in the region's political development.

Women's leadership

Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti

An educator and activist remembered for women's rights and opposition to colonial-era abuses.

Pre-colonial ruler

Queen Amina of Zazzau

A celebrated ruler in Hausa historical tradition, associated with military leadership and the expansion of Zazzau.

Historical Places in Nigeria

Explore places connected to Nigerian history, culture, archaeology and national memory.

Ile-Ife, Osun State

A historic Yoruba spiritual and cultural centre, renowned for its ancient art and traditions.

Benin City, Edo State

The historic centre of the Benin Kingdom, celebrated for its royal court and bronze-casting traditions.

Badagry, Lagos State

A major location for understanding the history of the transatlantic slave trade and its legacy.

Jos, Plateau State

Home to important archaeological discoveries and the National Museum, which houses Nok-related collections.

About this project

Preserving Our Shared Heritage

Nigeria History Explorer is an educational project designed to help students, teachers, researchers and curious readers discover the country's rich and diverse past.

History is complex. Dates, interpretations and traditions can sometimes differ between historical sources. By studying multiple sources, we can develop a deeper understanding of Nigeria's rich and diverse past.
